Who we are

Wello Partners ("Wello", "we", "our", or "us") operates a cashback and offer platform. Users can redeem offers, verify purchases, and receive cashback payouts.

What we collect

We collect information necessary to operate Wello, including:

  • Account and profile data: name, email, and account identifiers.
  • Offer and redemption data: redemptions, receipt uploads, business/offer metadata, and verification outcomes.
  • Bank verification data via Plaid: linked institution and account identifiers, account type/subtype, masked account details, and transaction data (such as merchant name, amount, and transaction dates) used for purchase verification.
  • Identity/ownership data via Plaid (when available): account owner name data used for fraud prevention and verification.
  • Payout and billing data via Stripe: Stripe account IDs, payout status, and payout metadata needed to process cashback payouts and platform billing.
  • Device and operational data: app logs, diagnostics, notification tokens, and approximate location data when needed for in-store redemption features.

We do not store your bank login credentials. We do not store full card numbers.

How we use information

  • Provide and secure the Wello app and user accounts.
  • Verify eligible purchases for cashback rewards.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and duplicate claims.
  • Process cashback payouts through Stripe.
  • Operate customer support, troubleshooting, and product improvements.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Plaid is used for verification and identity/ownership checks. Stripe is used for payouts and other money movement.

How we share information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers and vendors that support our platform, including Plaid, Stripe, and cloud infrastructure providers.
  • Authorities or legal counterparties where required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Successor entities in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets.

We do not sell personal information.

Data retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to operate the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No system is 100% secure, but we work to reduce risk and limit access to sensitive data.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and update profile information in the app.
  • Unlink linked bank accounts from Wello.
  • Request account deletion and associated data deletion, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Contact us with privacy requests or questions.

Children's privacy

Wello is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

US data storage

Wello stores and processes covered data in the United States. By using Wello, you consent to processing and storage in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and post the revised policy on this page.
