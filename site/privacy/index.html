Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wello Partners LLC ("Wello", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Wello website, Wello mobile applications, customer support channels, and related service operations.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, profile details, role data, and account security metadata.
  • Offer and redemption data: business listings, offers, redemptions, receipt uploads, reviews, and related status/history.
  • Verification and banking metadata: linked account metadata, masked financial account information, transaction-matching metadata, and related verification outcomes.
  • Billing and cashback metadata: business payment method metadata, commission and billing artifacts, cashback ledger events, and related operational status history.
  • Device and app operations data: app diagnostics, app version, push notification tokens, and usage events.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location data when needed for nearby business/offer discovery and location-powered app features.
  • Support communications: details you submit through support forms or support email.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate Wello services and account features.
  • Review and verify redemptions and related reward flows.
  • Run billing, fraud controls, and operational safeguards.
  • Send service notifications and product-related communications.
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

4. Service Providers and Third Parties

We use third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate Wello. These providers process data only as needed to provide services on our behalf.

  • Supabase: database, auth, storage, and backend services.
  • Stripe: business billing, invoicing, and payment method operations for business accounts.
  • Plaid: bank-link and transaction-verification support for eligible verification features.
  • Brevo: contact form and support email delivery.
  • Cloudflare: Turnstile abuse prevention for contact submissions and R2 object storage used for certain uploaded assets and receipt-related media flows.
  • Expo Push: push notification delivery infrastructure.
  • Apple Maps and Google Maps/Places services: map display, directions, and place lookup features.

Additional details are available in our Third-Party Notices.

5. How We Share Information

We may share information:

  • With service providers that support core platform operations.
  • When required by law, regulation, subpoena, or legal process.
  • To protect rights, safety, security, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • In connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, or asset transfer.

We do not sell personal information. We also do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as needed for service operations, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and enforcement of agreements. We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no system is fully immune from risk.

Data retention baseline

  • Account/profile data is retained while your account is active and as needed for account recovery, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.
  • Operational records (offers, redemptions, cashback activity, support records, and audit logs) are retained as needed for service operations, security, accounting, and legal obligations.

Plaid offboarding and revocation handling

  • If a user unlinks a bank or Plaid sends revocation webhooks, we stop using that connection for verification and clear active access token references from our linked-item records.
  • Linked account metadata associated with revoked access is marked inactive and no longer used for verification operations.

Deletion requests

You can request deletion of your account data by emailing support@wellopartners.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". After identity verification, we process deletion requests within a commercially reasonable timeframe, subject to records we are required to retain for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

7. Your Rights and Choices (US + California Baseline)

Depending on applicable law, including California law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of certain personal information, and request information about categories of personal information we collect, use, and disclose.

To submit a request, email support@wellopartners.com with the subject line "Privacy Request".

8. Children

Wello is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this policy periodically. If material changes are made, we will update the effective date and publish the revised version on this page.

10. Contact

Privacy or data requests: support@wellopartners.com
