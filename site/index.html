FAQ

How does business approval work?

New business accounts go through an admin review workflow before they are made live to users.

How are redemptions verified?

Wello supports verification through receipt review and bank-linked transaction matching where applicable.

How are disputes or support issues handled?

Businesses can contact support directly, and issues are triaged through operational review paths.

Can we edit or pause offers after publishing?

Yes. Offer details can be updated, paused, or reviewed again based on your account permissions and status.

What data is used for verification?

Verification uses relevant redemption and transaction context required to confirm eligibility and enforce controls.

How quickly can our business go live?

Time-to-live depends on account readiness and review completion. Support can help unblock requirements quickly.
